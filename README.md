# 🚲 London Bike Rides Analysis | Tableau Dashboard

## 📌 About the Project

In this project, I used Tableau to analyze London bike-sharing data and understand how people use bikes throughout the day and across different weather conditions.

I created an interactive dashboard to explore ride patterns, identify trends, and understand how factors like temperature, wind speed, and seasons are related to bike usage.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the overall number of bike rides.
- Identify the busiest hours for bike usage.
- Understand daily and seasonal ride patterns.
- Explore how weather conditions affect bike rides.
- Track ride trends using moving averages.

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

- **Total Bike Rides:** Shows the overall number of bike rides.
- **Hourly Analysis:** Helps identify peak hours and changes in ride demand throughout the day.
- **Moving Average:** Shows ride trends over time.
- **Weather Analysis:** Explores bike usage under different weather conditions.
- **Temperature vs. Wind Speed:** A heatmap showing how temperature and wind speed relate to bike ride demand.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Ride Demand:** Bike usage changes throughout the day, helping identify periods of higher and lower demand.
- **Hourly Trends:** The hourly analysis helps identify busy hours and understand daily travel patterns.
- **Seasonal Patterns:** Bike usage can be compared across different seasons to understand changes in demand.
- **Weather Impact:** Weather conditions play an important role in understanding variations in bike ride demand.
- **Temperature & Wind Speed:** The heatmap helps explore how temperature and wind speed are associated with bike usage.
- **Ride Trends:** The moving average makes it easier to identify overall trends and changes in bike demand over time.

These insights help understand bike-sharing usage patterns and support better planning of bike-sharing services in London.
